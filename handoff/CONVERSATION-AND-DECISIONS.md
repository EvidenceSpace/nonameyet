# Conversation and decisions

This is a structured record of product conversation. It preserves decisions and representative intent without private tool/session identifiers.

## Product origin

The user described a virtual case environment where people add material, connect it, receive AI guidance on what evidence or legal path may matter, collaborate, and contact support. The user corrected the order: **build the application before the promotional website**.

## Audience and platform

The product welcomes individuals, lawyers, investigators, small-case users, students, and educators. Adapt language, onboarding, templates, and explanation depth without weakening truth or permissions.

V1 is an online Windows and macOS desktop application. Local capability is recovery cache, not a separate offline collaboration promise.

## Core workspace

The user requested a broad connected app rather than every feature on one page. The accepted model separates Brief, Space, Evidence, Research, Work, Room, and Reports while linking canonical objects across them.

## Space

V1 requires 2D evidence/photo frames, notes, connectors, markers, frames, timeline, tasks, research assets, pins, comments, history, and AI proposals. 3D and sound are later candidates. Board elements must look buildable, attached, precise, and keyboard-accessible through a structured equivalent.

## AI

The desired posture is truthful, calm, professionally skeptical, source-backed, willing to show material against the user, and ending with a justified next step. This does not permit lawyer impersonation. AI sees authorized context, proposes changes, and acts only after review. Shared Case AI history is visible only to authorized members; private AI is not.

## Work, collaboration, and reports

Work is a first-class lens with title, requested information, status, priority, deadline, assignee, instructions, and sources. Room is human-first text collaboration with threads, mentions, pins, decisions, conversions, and optional shared Case AI. Reports preserve research, evidence index, chronology, contrary material, unknowns, tasks, decisions, and provenance; AI may draft but cannot approve, export, or send.

## Professional support

Find a Lawyer is Premium. Users search and compare verified professionals, open profiles, book, pay, and selectively share. Payment never grants case access. Consultation, engagement, and formal representation are separate states.

## Onboarding and notifications

A one-page onboarding was rejected as congested. The accepted flow uses Profile, Legal context, Guidance, Accessibility, and First case after account entry; the first story belongs to New Case.

The accepted notification lifecycle is Bell badge, focused toast or privacy-safe OS notice, origin-preserving drawer, All Notifications, and exact-object return. Notification preferences show no unread badge.

## Appearance and visual evolution

System follows Windows/macOS appearance automatically; explicit Light or Dark overrides it.

Rejected directions included disconnected impossible screens, overly dark boards, feature-congested pages, generic dashboards, Threaded Casebook, and generic chat intake. Signal Field / Case Space developed into the accepted connected 37-screen prototype.

On 30 August 2026 the user accepted the main design and idea while noting small bar/tab/connection errors to fix in implementation.

## 9 September 2026 correction

After packaged startup, the user rejected the generic thin shell as product UI. A subsequent standalone concept also drifted too far from the approved app. The user’s correction:

- the approved HTML remains the best design;
- improve it instead of redesigning it;
- preserve its color scheme, background character, geometry, navigation, and composition;
- make blank areas more engaging without filling them with generic cards;
- make vocabulary simple and understandable;
- use much less copy and smaller headings;
- make the result feel professionally human-designed;
- avoid AI-generated imagery and generic AI visual effects; and
- keep animation limited, purposeful, and designed for the exact interaction.

The standalone direction was rejected before any commit or pull request. Its technical cleanliness did not make it an accepted product design.

## Repository continuity decision

The user authorized routine branch, pull-request, and merge work without repeated permission and required durable project knowledge to live in the repository and reach `main` after review. Specifications, page/section plans, future ideas, implementation status, quality rules, visual references, and handoff must stay current as the project changes.

Durable automated tests stay with the code. Temporary test packages, private screenshots, observations, traces, recordings, machine paths, and rejected design artifacts stay out.

## Explicit exclusions and corrections

No coding competition, tournament, fight, duo/squad, game mode, outcome prediction, background surveillance, broad V1 connectors, V1 3D, required sound, mobile app, native calls, or autonomous external/destructive actions.

## Current mandate

Build a professional, coherent, prototype-faithful product through tested vertical slices. Do not restart settled discovery, imitate competitors, or sacrifice trust, accessibility, performance, provenance, recovery, or visual character for speed.
