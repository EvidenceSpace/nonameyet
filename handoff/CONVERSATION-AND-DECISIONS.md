# Conversation and decisions

This is a structured record of the prior product conversation. It preserves decisions and representative user language without exposing internal tool/session identifiers.

## Product origin

The user described a virtual case environment where people add material, connect it, receive AI guidance on what evidence or legal path may matter, collaborate, and contact support. The user then corrected the order: **build the application before the promotional website**.

## Audience

The product should not artificially narrow to one persona. Individuals, lawyers, investigators, small-case users, students and educators should receive the best appropriate experience. Adapt vocabulary, onboarding, templates and explanation depth; never weaken truth or permissions.

## Platform

Desktop V1, online, Windows and macOS. Internet is accepted because AI, collaboration, research and members require it. Local capability is recovery cache, not a separate offline collaboration promise.

## Core workspace

The user requested a broad connected app rather than every feature crowded into one page. The final model separates Brief, Space, Evidence, Research, Work, Room and Reports while linking canonical objects between them.

## Space

Required: 2D now; evidence/photo frames, sticky notes, connectors, markers, frames, timeline, tasks, online visual/research assets, pins, comments, history and AI proposals. 3D and sound are later candidates. Board elements must look buildable, attached and precise.

## AI

The desired posture was “truthful lawyer” quality: professionally skeptical, source-backed, willing to show material against the user, and ending with a justified next step. This does not permit lawyer impersonation. AI sees only authorized context, proposes tasks/research/plans/board/report changes, and acts only after review. Shared Case AI history is visible to authorized members; private AI is not.

## Work

Tasks include title, requested document/information, status, priority, deadline, assignee and instructions. Work became a first-class lens rather than being buried in Room.

## Collaboration

Chat is primary V1 collaboration. Members can discuss, thread, mention, pin, decide and link objects. Native calls are later. Workspace membership remains separate from case access.

## Reports

Reports include research, evidence index, chronology, contrary material, unknowns, tasks and decisions. AI may draft but cannot approve, export or send.

## Professional support

Find a Lawyer is Premium. Users discover, search and compare verified professionals, open a profile, book, pay and selectively share. Payment never grants case access. Consultation, engagement and representation are different states.

## Onboarding correction

A one-page onboarding was rejected as congested. Account creation comes first; Profile comes before Legal context; progress is separate pages; Guidance and Accessibility follow; the first story is entered in New Case.

## Notification correction

Early notification concepts mixed overlay and dedicated-page shells. Final lifecycle: badge, focused toast, privacy-safe OS notice, origin-preserving drawer, View All route, exact-object return. Notification preferences show no unread badge.

## Appearance correction

System follows Windows/macOS appearance automatically. Light and Dark are explicit overrides. The product is light-first but supports both.

## Visual evolution

Rejected directions included disconnected impossible-looking screens, overly dark boards, feature-congested pages, generic “vibecoded” dashboards, and a Threaded Casebook concept. The accepted direction developed from Signal Field / Case Space: calm, modern, visually polished, spacious, connected, and purpose-built for evidence work.

## Page approvals

Approved through iterative review: Global Home; Cases; Story Field; Brief; Space; Evidence; Research; Work; Room; Reports; Find a Lawyer; Lawyer Profile; Booking/Payment/Selective Sharing; Welcome/Auth/Recovery; five-page onboarding; Notifications v6; Personalization; Profile & account; complete Settings.

## Final acceptance

On 30 August 2026 the user accepted the main design and idea, noted small bar/tab/connection errors for implementation, and authorized preparing and merging comprehensive specs and handoff material to `main`.

## Explicit exclusions/corrections

No coding competition, tournament, fight, duo/squad or game mode. No outcome prediction. No background surveillance. No broad connectors, 3D, required sound, mobile, native calls or autonomous external/destructive actions in V1.
