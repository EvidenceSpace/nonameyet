## 2026-09-09 — First connected EvidenceSpace product slice

### Outcome

Implemented the first recognizable connected flow:

The connected route is **Home → Cases → C-03 Brief → E-04 Evidence → Context
Lens → exact-object return**.

The work refines the canonical prototype rather than replacing it. It uses one
persistent shell, query-addressable routes, History API navigation, route-level
native-module loading, exact case/evidence context, concise human copy,
proportional typography, restrained motion, and honest foundations for
unfinished destinations.

  AI, and quick starts.

- Source-qualified Brief with contrary material, unknowns, descriptive path, one
  source-linked next step, and connected case map.

  statements, backlinks, exact return, and session-only confirm/correct

  source-backed assessment, and `AI proposal · Not applied`.

  forced-colors behavior.

### Truth boundary

The slice uses synthetic fixtures. It does not implement production auth,
persistent or collaborative writes, evidence ingestion, AI execution,
marketplace, payment, notifications, or legal advice. Preview controls
explicitly avoid persistence claims. CaseFind remains the migration asset for
proven storage, integrity, review, timeline, export, deletion, and recovery
behavior.
