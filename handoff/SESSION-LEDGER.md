# Design and planning session ledger

## Product definition

- Application first; marketing site later.
- Online desktop V1 for Windows and macOS.
- Broad audience with adaptive onboarding.
- 2D case environment, source preservation, AI guidance, collaboration, reports and Premium lawyer discovery.

## Early design lessons

- First concepts were rejected as disconnected and infeasible.
- Dark deterministic boards improved structure but were too dark and concentrated.
- Light product-system exploration improved clarity but still felt generic.
- Threaded Casebook was rejected.
- Signal Field / Case Space established the accepted direction.
- Board element precision study was approved.

## Wider application approvals

- Global Home — approved.
- Cases Library — approved.
- Generic new-case chat — visually acceptable but considered overused; superseded by Story Field.
- Story Field — approved.
- Case Brief/Overview — approved.
- Evidence Library and Source Viewer — approved.
- Research first version had page/connection errors; corrected version approved.
- Work — approved after count/lens corrections.
- Room — approved.
- Reports — approved.
- Lawyer marketplace and profile — approved.
- Booking/Payment/Selective Sharing — approved.
- Authentication/Recovery — approved.

## Onboarding

Initial one-screen flow rejected as congested. Revised five-page flow approved: Profile, Legal context, Guidance, Accessibility, First case.

## Notifications

Multiple iterations corrected overlay/dedicated-route confusion and badge placement. V6 lifecycle approved with deferred animation polish.

## Settings

Personalization clarified System vs explicit Light/Dark. Profile/account approved. Complete Settings continuation approved, including AI guidance, notifications, privacy, security, members, connections, history, billing and support.

## Connected review

- Work count corrected to six open in the synthetic fixture.
- Research restored as the fourth case lens in the Board source/render.
- Connected application map passed 15 contract checks.
- Screenshot-backed end-to-end review included 37 routes and passed 32 contract checks.
- User accepted the design direction and deferred small shell/tab/connection defects to implementation.

## Phase transition

Design direction is complete. Next phase is repository readiness, CI truth, ADR spikes and production vertical slices. Motion studies remain quality work, not a prerequisite to start architecture/CI preparation.
