# Record readiness

Record readiness is a transparent local organization score from 0 to 100. It is not an AI prediction and does not measure legal strength, authenticity, credibility, fault, or likelihood of an outcome.

The score has four visible components:

- Collection checklist: 30 points across six record categories.
- Original records: 25 points, reaching full credit at three records.
- Verified facts: 30 points, reaching full credit at three confirmed or corrected facts with matching source provenance.
- Review completion: 15 points based on resolved suggestions. It contributes zero before any original record exists.

Suggested and uncertain facts never count as verified. Confirmed facts with missing records or mismatched source hashes receive no points and appear as a repair step. The browser recalculates from current IndexedDB state when records, checklist selections, facts, or suggestions change.
