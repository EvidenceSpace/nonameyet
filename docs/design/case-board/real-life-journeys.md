# Real-life journeys

Purpose: test whether the case board is useful in real life before building it. Each journey follows real people day by day and marks:

- what they would actually open
- what they need from us
- where they would quit
- what makes it worth paying for

The people are fictional. The situations are common. The owner asked for real situations from people they know to be added later. Real cases beat invented ones.

Legend: ✅ the design handles it · ⚠️ risk · ➕ needs design work

---

## 1. Sam: car accident, starts alone, brings in a solicitor

**Who:** Sam Reyes, 34, warehouse supervisor. Not legal-minded. Phone first, laptop at night.

| When | What happens | What Sam opens | What he needs | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Day 0, 08:50 | Crash at a junction. Shaken. | Phone, maybe the app if someone told him about it | A calm checklist: photos, other driver's details, witnesses, police ref, tell insurer | ➕ phone "it just happened" start |
| Day 0, evening | Wants to know "am I in trouble?" | App, home | Plain answer: what usually happens next and what to keep | ➕ first-run guidance per case type |
| Days 1–7 | Joan emails a statement; photos, repair estimate, police ref | Forwards emails, uploads photos | Suggestions to confirm in under a minute | ⚠️ confirming must feel quick and pay off at once |
| Day 17 | Insurer denies liability: "green light, speeding" | App, properly, on laptop | See what he has vs what they say, and what's missing | ✅ the board earns its place here |
| Day 17 | Wonders if he needs a lawyer | Lawyers | Compare, see cost, book a consultation | ✅ existing marketplace plan |
| Day 18 | Invites Dana into the case | Room, share | Dana sees an organised case in minutes | ✅ biggest value for lawyers |
| Weeks 3–8 | Waiting on Joan's file, the council plan, the medical report | Notifications, Room | Quiet reminders, chasing drafts, no fake activity | ➕ deadline and chase design |
| Week 9 | Letter of claim sent by Dana | Reports / Work | Letter built from the board, reviewed by Dana | ✅ prototype shows the core |

**Would he quit?** Yes, at two moments: if day 0 shows a complex board instead of help, or if days 1–7 feel like data entry.
**Would he pay?** Most likely when liability is denied (day 17) and when he wants a lawyer to take it on cheaply because the case is already organised.

## 2. Dana: solicitor receiving the case

**Who:** Dana Okafor, solicitor in a small firm, 40 open road-traffic files, uses a case management system already.

| When | What happens | What Dana opens | What she needs | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Invite | Sam shares the case | Email, then app | To see the case in 5 minutes: parties, points, gaps, deadlines | ✅ proof panel and board |
| First review | Spots that Ade stood behind the signals | Board, place view | Fast checks, comments pinned to cards | ✅ |
| Work | Needs a letter in her firm's format | Reports | Export to Word, her template, her name | ➕ export and templates |
| Ongoing | Has her own system | — | Not to re-enter everything twice | ⚠️ big risk: duplicate work |
| Billing | Time is money | — | Less time per file than her current process | ⚠️ must be clearly faster |

**Would she quit?** Yes, if the app makes her do anything twice or her firm can't keep its records.
**Would she pay?** Only through clients arriving already organised, plus an export that saves hours. A firm plan needs exports, audit trails and permissions.
**Design response:** a "client-organised case" handover pack (index, chronology, disputed points, documents) that exports cleanly. Don't try to replace her case management system in V1.

## 3. Priya: freelance designer, unpaid invoice (£2,400)

**Who:** Priya, freelancer. The client went quiet after delivery. Small claims territory.

| When | What happens | What Priya opens | What she needs | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Week 0 | Invoice 30 days overdue | App, laptop | "Can I get my money and how?" | ➕ light mode: checklist, next step, letter |
| Same day | Uploads contract, emails, invoice | Upload | Suggestions: terms, delivery date, no complaint received | ✅ |
| Same day | Wants a polite but firm reminder, then a letter before action | Work/Reports | Drafts using her facts | ✅ |
| Week 2 | No reply | Notifications | "14 days passed: next is a letter before claim" | ✅ deadline engine |
| Week 5 | Court claim | — | How to file online, what to attach | ➕ guidance, not filing |

**Would she quit?** If she has to learn a board for a £2,400 debt.
**Would she pay?** A small one-off fee for a solid letter and a ready bundle, if it feels fair.
**Design response:** light mode by default for small money claims, board optional.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move-out +10 days | Landlord keeps £600 for "cleaning and damage" | App on both phones | Shared case, both can add photos | ✅ team case |

## What the journeys prove

**Needed (high confidence):**
1. A helpful start per situation, phone-friendly for urgent ones.
2. Quick confirm with immediate visible payoff.
3. Proof panel, holes and one next step.
4. Deadlines that are correct and explained.
5. Room: talk, call, share with a lawyer, guest access for witnesses.
6. Outputs (letters, bundles) in the formats people actually need, exportable.
7. A light mode for small cases.

**Valuable but later:** place plans with sightlines, measuring from video, live cursors.

**Risks:** lawyers refusing duplicate work; wrong deadlines or weights; self-represented users never reaching the board; overwhelm on day one.

**When people pay:** when the other side says no (liability denied, invoice ignored, deposit kept); when they want a lawyer and want it cheaper; when they need a document they can send.
