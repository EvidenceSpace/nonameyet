# Checklist status in verified reports

When the collection checklist export option is enabled, the verified report includes all six record categories with their current user-controlled states: Found, Missing, Needs review, or Not applicable.

The report does not silently reduce the checklist to found items. Showing missing and needs-review categories prevents an incomplete collection from appearing complete, while not-applicable categories remain distinguishable from missing records.

Checklist states are organizational labels supplied by the user. They do not authenticate a file, prove that evidence is sufficient, or determine whether a category is legally required. CaseFind does not infer these states from filenames, AI suggestions, or facts.

Existing cases using the earlier binary checklist remain compatible. Checked categories render as Found and the remaining standard categories render as Missing.

Users may exclude the entire checklist section during export. The option applies equally to preview and download. Checklist data never introduces original file bytes or an additional network request.
