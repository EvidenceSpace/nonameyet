# CaseFind V1 product specification

## Product promise

Explain what happened, upload related client messages and documents, and CaseFind will help identify important facts, missing information, and conflicts before producing a verified, source-linked case record.

## Initial user

A freelancer or small service provider with unpaid work whose records are distributed across screenshots, invoices, proposals, receipts, PDFs, and delivery messages.

## V1 user journey

1. Create an unpaid-work case and describe the desired outcome.
2. Receive the standard unpaid-work collection checklist.
3. Upload screenshots, images, and PDFs.
4. Extract candidate dates, amounts, participants, facts, and events.
5. Confirm, correct, dismiss, or mark each important item uncertain.
6. Review missing checklist items and conflicts across sources.
7. Browse a chronological, source-linked timeline.
8. Preview a case record, pay for an export, or permanently delete the case.

## Case workspace

- **Overview:** amount, date range, organizational readiness, unresolved conflicts, and missing information.
- **Checklist:** found, missing, needs review, not applicable.
- **Files:** originals, safe previews, categories, metadata, and processing status.
- **Facts:** structured claims with exact source references and verification state.
- **Timeline:** user-editable events with supporting sources.
- **Gaps and conflicts:** missing information and contradictory extracted values.

## V1 file support

- PNG, JPEG, and HEIC screenshots or photographs.
- PDFs containing invoices, proposals, email printouts, and agreements.
- Maximum 100 files per case and 20 MB per file for the initial beta.

## Required trust language

CaseFind organizes user-provided material. It does not authenticate files, determine legal rights, provide legal advice, or predict outcomes.

## Explicitly excluded

- Full-device access or background monitoring.
- Direct SMS, call-log, WhatsApp, or mailbox access.
- Court filing and jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
- Screenshot-authenticity or forensic-certification claims.
- Family, custody, criminal, or emergency workflows.
- Audio and video analysis.

## V1 success criteria

- Users can locate the source of every confirmed fact.
- Users understand the difference between extracted, suggested, and confirmed information.
- A complete case can be permanently deleted without support intervention.
- Beta users finish verification faster than producing the same record manually.
- Some beta users are willing to pay for a polished export after seeing the full preview.
